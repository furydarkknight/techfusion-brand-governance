Judging Criteria & Feature Alignment (100 Points)

1. Working Core Workflow (30 Points) 100% Operational

End-to-end journey from content entry → 5-pillar brand audit → explainable findings with rule links → purpose-preserved corrections → editorial review & sign-off.

2. Technical Depth & Reliability (25 Points) Sub-15ms Local Core

FastAPI backend, deterministic lexical tokenizer + Flesch readability NLP heuristics, normalized schema with immutable audit logs, zero cloud latency.

3. Progress from Round 1 (20 Points) All 3 Phases Built

Built Guideline Hub (Phase 1), Core Inspection Engine with 1-click diffs (Phase 2), and Role-based Approvals with Override tracking (Phase 3).

4. Three Round 3 Planned Improvements (15 Points) Substantial Roadmap

Multi-Modal Vision Inspector (PDF/Image checks), Vector-Grounded Semantic RAG for Brand Books, and Real-Time CMS / Google Docs extensions.

Three Major Improvements Scheduled for Round 3

Improvement 1: Multi-Modal Computer Vision Brand Inspector

Automated computer vision inspection for marketing banners, infographics, PDFs, and slide decks. The engine will perform automated OCR, logo safe-zone measurement, brand font hierarchy verification, and WCAG 2.1 AA foreground-to-background contrast ratio calculation directly on uploaded image files (PNG/JPEG/SVG/PDF).

Improvement 2: Vector-Grounded Semantic RAG & Dynamic Brand Guidelines Ingestion

Integration of ChromaDB/FAISS vector retrieval pipeline allowing enterprise admins to upload unstructured PDF brand books, annual SEC 10-K filings, and press releases. The system chunks and indexes verified facts and tone rules with semantic similarity search, providing context-aware explanations for nuanced industry jargon.

Improvement 3: Real-Time CMS & Browser Extensions (Figma, Google Docs, Chrome)

Inline checking extensions for Google Docs, WordPress, and Figma. Creators will receive non-intrusive brand guardrail prompts while drafting directly inside their native design and authoring tools, eliminating the friction of manual copy-pasting.
